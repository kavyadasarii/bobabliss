2. Placing an order

Choosing a drink and tapping "Place order" sends us a request to prepare it. We may decline or cancel an order, for example if an item has run out, if we are about to close, or if we cannot reach you using the phone number you gave. If that happens we will let you know as soon as we can.

Please give a correct name and phone number. We use them to contact you about your order.

3. Prices and payment

  • Prices are shown in Indian rupees (₹) and are for the size, toppings and quantity you choose.
  • You can pay at the store (cash, card or UPI) when you collect, or choose to pay by UPI using a link we text to you.
  • If you pay by UPI link, your order is confirmed once the payment goes through.
  • Prices and menu items can change. The price shown in your order summary when you place the order is the price you pay.

4. Pickup and timing

  • Orders are for pickup at the counter at Road No. 12, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad 500034. We do not offer delivery through this website.
  • "As soon as possible" orders are usually ready in about 15 minutes. This is an estimate, and busy times can take longer.
  • Scheduled orders can be set for any time from 11:00 to 21:30 on a date you choose, at least 15 minutes from the time you order. We are open daily from 11:00 to 22:00.
  • Boba drinks taste best fresh. Please collect your order soon after it is ready. If an order is not collected within a reasonable time, we may discard it and may not be able to refund it.

5. Changes and cancellations

Drinks are made to order, so please contact us as quickly as you can by phone if you want to change or cancel. We can usually change or cancel an order that we have not started making. Once a drink has been made, we cannot cancel it.

6. Allergens and dietary needs

The notes box on the order form is a helpful way to tell us about an allergy, but it is not a guarantee that we can prepare a drink free of an allergen. If you have a serious allergy, please speak to our staff at the counter before you order.

Allergen information on this website is provided in good faith. Always check with staff if you have a medical dietary requirement.
