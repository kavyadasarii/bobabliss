This policy explains what information BobaBliss collects when you use our website to order bubble tea for pickup, and what we do with it. We keep it to what we need to make and hand over your drink.

1. Who we are

BobaBliss is a bubble tea shop at Road No. 12, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad 500034. When this policy says "we", "us" or "our", it means BobaBliss. For questions about your information, see Contact us.

2. What we collect

When you place an order we ask for:

  • Your name, so we can call it out at the counter.
  • Your phone number, so we can reach you about your order and send a payment link if you choose to pay by UPI.
  • Your order: drinks, sizes, sweetness, ice, milk, toppings and quantities.
  • Pickup time, either as soon as possible or a date and time you choose.
  • Notes you add, such as special requests or allergies.
  • Payment choice: pay at the store or by UPI link.

We do not ask for card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords on this website.

3. How we use it

  • To prepare your drinks and have them ready at the time you chose.
  • To contact you about your order, for example if an item is unavailable.
  • To send a payment link by SMS when you choose to pay by UPI.
  • To keep records of orders, handle refunds or complaints, and meet legal and tax obligations.
  • To keep the website working and secure.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Payments

If you pay at the store, payment happens at the counter. If you choose UPI, we text you a link that opens your own payment app, and the payment is handled by your UPI app and your bank. We do not receive or store your UPI PIN or bank login details.

5. Allergy notes

Allergy or dietary details you write in the notes box can be sensitive. We use them only to prepare your order safely, and we do not use them for marketing. Please also tell the staff at the counter, because our kitchen handles milk, soy, nuts and other common allergens.

6. Sharing

We share your information only when needed to run the service:

  • With staff at our store who prepare and hand over your order.
  • With service providers that help us, such as SMS delivery or website hosting, and only for those purposes.
  • With authorities when the law requires it.

7. Browser storage

Your browser may store small pieces of data from this site, for example your light or dark theme and your text direction (left-to-right or right-to-left), so the site looks the way you left it. A drink you design on the Customize page is held temporarily in your browser so it can appear in your order, and is cleared once it has been added. You can clear this data at any time in your browser settings.

8. How long we keep it

We keep order details only as long as needed to fulfil your order, deal with any issue that follows, and meet our record-keeping duties. After that we delete them or remove anything that identifies you.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your information, where we are not required to keep it.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm it is really you before we act on it.

10. Children

Our online ordering is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place the order for you.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No website or message can be guaranteed completely secure, so please avoid putting sensitive details in the notes box beyond what we need to prepare your drink.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we will make it clear on the website.
