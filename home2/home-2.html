Born in the night markets, made for your neighbourhood

Bubble tea began in the lively night markets of Taiwan. We bring that playful, share-with-everyone spirit to every cup and focus on doing the basics well.

  • Small-batch tea. Brewed through the day, never from concentrate.
  • Pearls cooked in-store. Soft, chewy and still warm when they reach your cup.
  • Real fruit. Fruit teas and smoothies are made with fruit, not syrup alone.
  • A welcoming space. Come for the drink, stay for the good company.

Questions, answered

Everything people ask us most at the counter. Can’t find yours? We’re happy to help.

Contact us
Can I change the sweetness, ice and milk?

Yes. Every drink is a starting point. Choose sweetness from 0% to 100%, your ice level, and fresh, oat, soy or non-dairy creamer.

Do you have caffeine-free options?

Our smoothies have no tea base, and the Blueberry Hibiscus Cooler is caffeine-free too. You can also pick “no tea” when you build your own drink.

How much notice do you need for a group order?

We ask for 24 hours’ notice and an order of 5 cups or more. Send the date, pickup time and headcount through the Contact page.

What are your opening hours?

Every store is open daily from 11:00 to 22:00, with fresh batches of pearls cooked through the day.

I have an allergy. What should I do?

Please tell our crew when you order, or add a note to your group order, and we will help you choose a suitable drink.
