Born in the night markets, made for your neighbourhood

Bubble tea started in the lively night markets of Taiwan, where friends shared a cup as easily as a conversation. We bring that same playful, welcoming spirit to every BobaBliss store.

We keep the menu simple to love and easy to change. Start with a favourite or build your own, and we will make it fresh to your order.

  • Small-batch tea. Brewed through the day, never from concentrate.
  • Pearls cooked in-store. Soft, chewy and still warm when they reach your cup.
  • Real fruit. Fruit teas and smoothies are made with fruit, not syrups alone.

What drives us

Our mission is what we do every day. Our vision is where we are headed.

Our mission

To make every cup of bubble tea fresh, personal and joyful, brewed from real tea, finished with real pearls and made exactly the way you like it.

  • Brew tea fresh in small batches all day.
  • Let every guest choose their sweetness, ice, milk and toppings.
  • Serve each drink with a friendly smile and a clean cup.

Our vision

To be the neighbourhood bubble tea shop people trust and love coming back to, a place where good drinks bring people together in every city we serve.

  • Set the standard for quality in bubble tea.
  • Build stores that feel like a second living room.
  • Grow carefully, keeping every cup as good as the first.

From leaf to cup

Every drink follows the same four steps, whichever store you visit.

  1. Brew the tea

    Loose-leaf black, jasmine green or oolong is steeped fresh through the day.

  2. Cook the pearls

    Tapioca pearls are cooked in-store in small batches so they stay soft and chewy.

  3. Shake or blend

    Your tea, milk, fruit and sweetness are shaken or blended to order.

  4. Top and serve

    Toppings go in, the cup is sealed and your drink is handed over fresh.
